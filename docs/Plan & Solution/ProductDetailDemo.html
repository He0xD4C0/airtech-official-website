Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EC centrifugal fan? +
An EC centrifugal fan combines a centrifugal impeller with an electronically commutated motor, providing high efficiency and variable-speed control.
Can the fan speed be controlled? +
Yes. Depending on the configuration, the fan can support 0-10V or PWM speed control.
Can AIRTEK customize the fan? +
Yes. AIRTEK can provide customization for voltage, control interface, impeller configuration, housing, connectors and other OEM requirements.
