## Publication template

Before publishing a case claim, capture:

- Approved public title and anonymization level.
- Customer/industry/application and permission status.
- Problem, baseline, product/configuration, and engineering intervention.
- Operating point, environment, duration, sample size, and test method.
- Result with unit, uncertainty/tolerance, and comparison basis.
- Evidence owner, report link, approval date, expiry/review date, and allowed channels/languages.

If any required field is missing, write qualitative, carefully attributed copy or hold the claim for review; do not fill gaps from inference.
