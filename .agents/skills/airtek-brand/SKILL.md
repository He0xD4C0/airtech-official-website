# AIRTEKPOWER Brand

Use the normalized company and brand baseline without reproducing legacy residue from the source files.

## Route the task

- Always read [identity-and-copy.md](references/identity-and-copy.md) for company naming or public copy.
- Read [visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) for logo, color, typography, components, or UI review.
- Read [claim-guardrails.md](references/claim-guardrails.md) for contacts, certifications, company metrics, slogans, case claims, or any publication-ready content.
- If a source disagrees with these references or a new value must be made canonical, also load `$airtek-knowledge-governance`.
- For product facts, load `$airtek-product-knowledge`; for page strategy, SEO, RFQ, analytics, or architecture, load `$airtek-website-growth`.

## Hard rules

- Write the brand as `AIRTEKPOWER` with no space. Use the normalized legal-entity candidate only where formal context requires it and after current owner/legal verification.
- Never reproduce the legacy names `Longwell`, `Longway`, `Longwei`, or `朗伟`.
- Use approved source logo artwork when available; do not redraw or approximate the mark.
- Do not present provisional company metrics, certifications, delivery promises, personal contacts, or case results as verified.
- Preserve the distinction between the durable mission/vision/values, the primary brand line, and a secondary campaign line.
- Prefer precise industrial language over generic superlatives. Do not invent “leading,” “best,” “certified worldwide,” or similar claims. The exact official Vision may retain “global leader” only when clearly labeled as the Vision, never as proof of current market leadership.

## Output check

Before handing off brand-facing work, verify spelling, legal name, colors, typography, email formatting, claim status, copyright year behavior, and consistency across desktop/mobile or language variants.
