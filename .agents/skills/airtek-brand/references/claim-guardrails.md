# Public claim guardrails

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14

## Safe baseline

The normalized identity, mission, vision, values, general emails, website, and address in [identity-and-copy.md](identity-and-copy.md) may be used as the drafting baseline. Re-check legal status, address, domain ownership, and mailbox ownership/routing before a production launch or formal publication.

## Verify before publication

The supplied decks report the following, but they are `PROVISIONAL` until an owner supplies a current claim record and scope:

- `100+` staff.
- `45000+` floor space; the source omits the unit, so do not infer one.
- `1,000,000+ pcs` annual output.
- `100+` global branding clients.
- `30+` R&D technical employees.
- `20+` certifications.
- `10+ years` of experience or operation, because the effective year and scope can drift.
- ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, CE, and ETL/UL coverage.

For certifications, verify certificate holder, standard/version, issuing body, validity, product/site scope, model coverage, market, and permission to display marks. Never imply that every product carries every listed certification.

The ISO 9001:2015 image recovered from the legacy public site is `DEPRECATED` because its displayed validity ended 2026-03-30. It is historical evidence, not publication artwork; see [brand-assets.md](brand-assets.md).

## Contacts and privacy

- Use `sales@airtekpower.com` or `marketing@airtekpower.com` according to intent.
- Do not publish names, personal email addresses, direct telephone numbers, or messaging accounts taken from example slides without explicit approval.
- Do not fabricate local offices, distributors, support hours, response times, or emergency contacts.

## Performance, sustainability, and cases

- Tie technical claims to the exact model, operating point, test method, baseline, environment, and date.
- Avoid universal statements such as “lowest energy,” “maintenance-free,” “zero failure,” or “guaranteed savings.”
- Treat reported case metrics and delivery lead times as provisional; use `$airtek-product-knowledge` for their recorded context.
- Do not identify masked competitors or clients.

## Copy review checklist

1. Is the company/brand name exact?
2. Is every number accompanied by unit, scope, and source?
3. Could the claim have expired?
4. Does certification language match the exact product and market?
5. Does a named customer, person, or competitor require permission?
6. Is a proposal being presented as an existing capability?
7. Is a translation preserving meaning rather than inventing an official phrase?
