## Voice

Write for industrial buyers and engineers: clear, evidence-led, concise, and technically literate. Lead with the application problem, relevant capability, integration value, and next action. Prefer concrete nouns and measurable conditions. Avoid unsupported market leadership, absolute performance, vague sustainability claims, and ornamental jargon.
